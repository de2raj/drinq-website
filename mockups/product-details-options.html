About this whisky

The name Chivas Regal is derived from Schivas Castle on the River Ythan in the Scottish province of Aberdeenshire. Chivas Regal Scotch 12 Years Old blends many malts and grain Scotch whiskies. A maturation of at least 12 years is required to bottle this blend with style, substance, and tradition. Chivas 12 is smooth, rich and generous.
